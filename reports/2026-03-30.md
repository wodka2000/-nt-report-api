per i clienti vulnerabili  
**Temi:** ⚡ Energia  
**Angolo normativo:** ARERA  
**Autore:** @wodka00  

📌 ARERA

On 30 March 2026, ARERA announced an 8.1% increase in the regulated electricity tariff (Maggior Tutela) for vulnerable customers in Q2 2026.

This update affects approximately 3 million vulnerable customers currently served under the Maggior Tutela regime. The adjustment is driven by rising wholesale energy prices on international markets, partly linked to ongoing uncertainty surrounding the Middle East conflict. The increase in energy supply costs is only partially offset by a 2.2% reduction in the dispatching price (PD).

System charges (oneri di sistema) remain unchanged for now, supported by optimised management of currently available liquidity. Additionally, in implementation of the DL Bollette, ARERA has introduced a reduction in the ASOS tariff component applied to energy withdrawn by non-domestic, non-energy-intensive users.

The estimated annual expenditure for a typical vulnerable customer under Maggior Tutela will reach €589.34 for the period 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026, representing a 4.5% increase compared to €563.76 recorded in the previous equivalent period. ARERA also reminds that all vulnerable customers currently in the free market are entitled to transition to the Maggior Tutela regime.
